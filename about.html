🎯 Who I Am & What I Do

In today's digital marketing landscape, strict privacy regulations, Safari's Intelligent Tracking Prevention (ITP), and widespread ad blockers cause businesses to lose 30%+ of vital conversion data. Consequently, ad platforms like Meta and Google optimize campaigns against incomplete metrics, wasting advertising budgets on unqualified traffic.

I bridge this reporting gap by moving fragile front-end tracking to robust, secure server-side architectures. My pipelines ensure that 100% of your user conversions and lead milestones are accurately captured, hashed securely, and routed directly to ad networks and CRMs.
