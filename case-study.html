Case Study #01 — E-commerce & Server-Side Tracking

Recovering 35%+ Lost Ad Signals via Cloudflare sGTM & Meta CAPI

Zero Monthly Hosting Fees

The Challenge & Problem

An expanding e-commerce brand was running heavy ad campaigns on Meta and Google. However, due to Safari ITP restrictions and aggressive ad-blockers, over 35% of checkout events were disappearing on the front end.

Ad platforms were optimizing against incomplete metrics, driving up the Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) while creating severe data mismatches inside GA4.

The Technical Solution

I engineered a custom server-side tracking architecture using Cloudflare Workers (bypassing expensive platform fees like Stape or GCP).

Integrated Meta CAPI and Google Ads OCT with SHA-256 PII data hashing, browser-server event deduplication, and extended cookie lifespans up to 730 days.

Case Study #02 — B2B & Lead Pipeline

Fixing GoHighLevel & Offline Conversion Tracking for SaaS

Revenue-Driven Attribution

The Challenge & Problem

A B2B lead generation client using GoHighLevel was losing critical UTM parameters inside iFrames. Furthermore, phone calls and offline sales qualified leads ("Closed Won") were never communicated back to Google Ads or Meta.

Ad algorithms had no visibility into actual closed revenue, making it impossible to scale profitable campaigns.

The Technical Solution

Built custom iFrame form scripting to capture `gclid`, `fbclid`, and UTMs reliably. Configured CallRail and Twilio integration to track manual phone calls.

Streamlined webhook logic via Zapier/n8n to push offline lifecycle stages directly to ad networks, training algorithms on genuine high-value buyers.
